AiNKI for Firefox

Connect the extension to your account. AiNKI asks you to allow it, the same way it does for Claude.

Connected to .

Select text on any page and press Alt+Shift+A, or right-click the selection or an image and pick Add to AiNKI. Nothing is saved until you press Add card or Ctrl+Enter.

Change the shortcut under Firefox's add-ons page, ⚙ → Manage Extension Shortcuts. Disconnecting ends the connection in AiNKI too (Settings → Connected apps).
